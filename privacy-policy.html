Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026 · Last updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hafiz Aqib ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the mobile apps and games we publish on Google Play and the Apple App Store (each an "App", together the "Apps"). It applies to every App that links to this page.

In short: We do not ask you for, or directly collect, personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. Our Apps have no user accounts. However, our Apps use third-party services for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, and in-app purchases, and those services automatically collect certain device and usage information as described below.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

None. Our Apps do not require registration, and we do not ask you to enter personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

Information collected automatically by third-party services

The third-party software development kits (SDKs) built into our Apps may automatically collect the following when you use an App:

  • Device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID, Apple Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), Identifier for Vendors (IDFV), and app instance IDs.
  • Device and technical information such as device model, operating system and version, language, screen size, mobile carrier, and app version.
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate (country or city-level) location. Our Apps do not request precise GPS location.
  • Usage and gameplay data such as sessions, screens viewed, levels played, progress events, and how you interact with ads.
  • Diagnostics such as crash logs, performance data, and error reports.
  • Purchase information such as which in-app item was bought and the transaction status (but never your payment card details).

Game progress and settings may also be stored locally on your device. That data stays on your device and is not sent to us.

3. How information is used

Information collected through our Apps is used to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Apps.
  • Show advertisements, including personalized ads where you have consented or where law permits.
  • Measure ad performance and prevent ad fraud.
  • Understand how players use the Apps so we can improve gameplay, balance, and features.
  • Detect, diagnose, and fix crashes and technical problems.
  • Process and verify in-app purchases and restore previous purchases.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information for money, and we do not use the data to make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. Sharing of information

We do not share information with anyone except:

  • Service providers listed in Section 2, who process data to provide their services to us.
  • Legal requirements, when required by law, regulation, or valid legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of our users or others.
  • Business transfers, if an App is sold or transferred. In that case this policy continues to apply to information collected before the transfer.

We only use third-party services that maintain privacy and security protections consistent with this policy and with Google Play and Apple App Store requirements.

5. Advertising & your choices

Our free Apps are supported by advertising through Google AdMob. Ads may be personalized based on your device's advertising identifier and your activity across apps and websites, or non-personalized (based only on context, such as the App you are using).

Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the App will ask for your consent through a consent form before personalized ads are shown or data is used for those purposes. You can change your choice at any time from the App's privacy or settings option, where available, or by reinstalling the App.

iOS – App Tracking Transparency

On iOS 14.5 and later, our Apps will ask your permission before accessing your IDFA or tracking you across apps and websites owned by other companies. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized using your IDFA. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Android – Advertising ID

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads, depending on your device).

Other opt-outs

You can learn more about how Google uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and manage ad personalization at adssettings.google.com.

6. In-app purchases

Some Apps offer optional in-app purchases (for example, removing ads or unlocking content). All payments are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never receive or store your payment card details, billing address, or other financial information. We receive only the information needed to confirm and deliver your purchase, such as a transaction or order ID and the product purchased.

7. Data retention & deletion

We do not operate our own servers that store your personal information. Data collected by third-party services is kept according to their own retention policies, for example:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase: user-level data is kept for up to 14 months (or as configured), then deleted automatically.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash data is kept for 90 days.
  • GameAnalytics and AdMob: kept according to their published policies linked above.

How to delete your data:

  • Uninstalling an App removes all data stored locally on your device, including game progress and settings.
  • Resetting your device's advertising identifier (see Section 5) breaks the link between your device and previously collected advertising data.
  • You may contact us at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} to request deletion of analytics data associated with your device. Because we do not know who you are, we may ask for details that help us identify your device's data (such as the App name and the approximate date you used it). We will respond within 30 days and forward deletion requests to our analytics providers where they support it.

8. Security

Data sent by our Apps to third-party services is encrypted in transit using industry-standard protocols (HTTPS/TLS). While we and our providers take reasonable measures to protect information, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. Children's privacy

Our Apps are intended for a general audience and are not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided information through one of our Apps, please contact us at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} and we will take steps to delete it.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information, including the right to:

  • Access the personal information held about you, or get a copy of it.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Delete your information.
  • Object to or restrict processing, including for personalized advertising.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing done before you withdrew it.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

EEA / UK (GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies, the legal bases for processing are your consent (personalized ads and, where required, analytics) and our legitimate interests in operating, securing, and improving the Apps (crash reporting, non-personalized ads, and fraud prevention).

California (CCPA/CPRA) and other US states

We do not sell personal information for money. Sharing device identifiers with advertising partners for personalized ads may be considered "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under some US state laws. You can opt out by turning off personalized ads on your device (see Section 5) or by contacting us. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

To exercise any of these rights, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.
